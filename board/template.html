Scouting Board

Every one of the 1,025 Pokémon, turned into a hockey player. Base stats, height, weight and type become 14 hockey skills, and those skills become a rating at center, wing, defense and goal. Pick anyone to see how they grade out.

# Pokémon Type Fit C W D G

The league

Thirty-two Gym Leader teams in four regional divisions and two conferences, the same shape as the NHL. Each team carries its leader's type as its identity.

How stats become skills

Each skill blends a few base stats. Height and weight are converted onto the base-stat scale first, so a heavy Pokémon checks harder and a tall one covers more net. Stats past 130 count at half rate, so outliers like Blissey's HP or Shuckle's defenses still help without breaking the scale. A blend of 20 maps to a skill of 0 and 150 maps to 100.

Type flavour

Types add small nudges on top of the stats. Ice types skate better, Fire types tire faster on the ice, and Psychic types read the play.

What each position values

A position rating is a weighted average of the skills below. A Pokémon's fit is the position where it rates highest.
